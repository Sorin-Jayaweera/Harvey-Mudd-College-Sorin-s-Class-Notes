## Postulates of QM

1) Linearity 
The state of a system is completely specified by a vector in Hilbert space
we have a ket like $\ket{\psi}$ or $\ket{\phi}$ where $\braket{ \psi | \psi }=1$
$\ket{\chi}=a\ket{\psi}+b\ket{\phi}$
$\bra{\chi}= \bra{\psi}a^{*}+\bra{\phi}b^{*}$

2) Physical observables correspond to Hermitian operators
$\hat{x},\hat{P},\hat{H},\hat{J}_{x},\hat{J}_{y},\hat{J}_{z}, \hat{J}^{2}$
$\hat{A}^{\dagger}=\hat{A}$

States of definite $\hat{A}$ are eigenstates of $\hat{A}$ with eigenvalue $a$, which is what you would measure

3) Symmetries (rotation, translation)
Correspond to unitary operators. These operators have an algebra with commutation relations  identical to classical symmetries/transformations

Commutations tell us how to combine symmetries, etc, but is not QM - its classical geometrical stuff. That is imported into the symmetries of unitary operators. Rotations are the only nontrivial things. 

4) Each symmetry is generated by a Hermitian operator connected to its Nother pair
