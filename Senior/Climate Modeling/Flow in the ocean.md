Deep currents (slow)
* Density differences
* Mostly horizontal, a tiiiny bit of vertical flow


Equation of state (temperature, salinity, pressure)

$$
\begin{align}
\rho = f(T,S,P)
\end{align}
$$
Density is a function of temp, saln, press.

$$
\begin{align}
\rho(T,S) = \rho_{0} (1- \alpha(T-T_{0}) + \beta(S-S_{0}))
\end{align}
$$
This is linearized for thermal expansion and Haline contraction.
